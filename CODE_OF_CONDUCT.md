# Code of Conduct

Be nice. It's a calculator.

- Respect others, even when you disagree.
- Criticise code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussions constructive and on topic.

**What happens if someone causes trouble?**

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or reject contributions when necessary.

If something serious happens, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.

That's it. Be decent to each other!
